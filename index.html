Hello Trenchess

"From the trench, the Chessmen grow stronger." The world cracks into terrain, and every piece learns a new way to fight.

  • Deploy: place up to 16 terrain tiles in your half and arrange your army in your back four rows.
  • Orders: in Command mode you give 2 orders a turn, each to a different unit. Combos are deadly: a discovered attack can take a king within one turn.
  • Terrain: forests stop rooks and knights, swamps stop bishops and knights, mountains stop rooks and bishops. Deserts let anyone in but stop sliders, and units left idle there at the end of your turn perish.
  • The Chessmen grow stronger: the Equinox King's Somersault Assault (2 straight, capturing what it passes), the Sacred Queen's 8 Legged Gallop (knight leaps), the Twilight Fortress's Four-Corners Fortnight (1 diagonal), the Light Seer's Leap of Faith (2 straight), the Shadow Knight's Triple-Bar Jump (3 straight) and the Nimbus Jumper's Double-back Flip (2 back).
  • Victory: capture or checkmate the enemy King.
  • Controls: click a unit, then a highlighted tile. Right-click cancels. Q/E rotate the board, T switches piece style, scroll zooms, right-drag pans, Space ends your turn.
